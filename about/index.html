About Departed Digital

A calmer way to close online accounts after a loss.

Departed Digital exists because the online side of a death is harder than most people expect. Social media profiles, email accounts and other services stay open long after someone has died. Families are left to work through platform forms, document requests and repeated chasing, at the worst possible time.

Why it exists

Built from a simple but important problem.

The service grew from a real experience close to home. We saw how hard it is when someone's accounts stay live after they have died, and how little clear, practical help there is for families who just want it done properly.

  • No passwords required
  • Short first step for families
  • Documents asked for later, not at the start
  • A written case record at the end
What the service is

We do the work for you.

Departed Digital handles the platform side for families in the UK and abroad. We send the removal or memorialisation requests, ask for the right documents at the right stage, and keep a clear record of what has been sent and what has been resolved.

The point is not more admin. The point is to take the task off your hands, so it does not sit unfinished for months.

Closing unused accounts is also a small kindness to the internet: less clutter, and less data held about someone who has gone.

What it is not

Not another website that fills in forms.

This is not another portal or a cold piece of software. You start with the basics, pay when you are ready, and only deal with sensitive documents once the case is under way.

We never ask for passwords or direct access to an account. We use each platform's own process for bereaved families.

The founder

Steven Kong

Departed Digital is run by Steven Kong from London. He started it after seeing, close to home, how long a person's accounts stay live after they die, and how little practical help there is for the family left to deal with them.

Steven is responsible for every guide on this site. Each one is checked against the platform's own process before it is published and again when that process changes. If you find something that is out of date, email him and he will put it right.

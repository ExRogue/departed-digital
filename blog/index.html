Free guides to closing online accounts after a death

Facebook, Instagram, Apple, Google, email, subscriptions and the post, written for families doing it themselves, and what to do when a request is not working.

These guides exist because the online side of a death is harder than anyone warns you. Each one is written for a family doing it themselves, checked against the platform's own process before it is published, and rechecked when that process changes. There is nothing to buy on any of them.

If you have not started, begin with the complete guide, which explains the two documents you need and the right order to work in. If you have already sent a request and nothing has happened, go straight to the pages for when a request is not working. And if you would rather hand the whole thing over, start a case and we will do it for you, from £149.

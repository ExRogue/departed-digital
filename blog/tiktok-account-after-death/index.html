How to Close a Deceased Person's TikTok Account

By Departed Digital · 6 min read · Updated August 2026

TikTok is often the platform families feel least equipped to deal with. Unlike Facebook, which has a clear public form for reporting a death, TikTok's process is harder to find and involves more waiting. Meanwhile the account keeps appearing in feeds, keeps gaining followers or comments, and in painful cases keeps being duetted or stitched by strangers.

This guide explains the routes that actually work, what TikTok will ask for, and one important step to take before the account disappears.

Save the Videos First

Once TikTok removes an account, the videos go with it, and TikTok does not offer families a download of a deceased person's content. If any of the videos matter to the family, save them before you report the death:

  • Public videos can usually be saved from the app using the Save video option on each one, unless the person had downloads disabled.
  • Where downloads are disabled, a screen recording from any phone preserves the video and sound.
  • If a family member knows the account login, the account's own data, including private videos and drafts on the device, can be backed up before any request is made.

This step feels heavy for many families, and it is one of the tasks we most often handle on their behalf. But do not skip it: removal is permanent.

What TikTok Does With a Deceased Person's Account

TikTok can memorialise an account once it learns the account holder has died, adding a "Remembering" label and preventing anyone from logging in or posting. Families can also request that the account be removed entirely. Which outcome you get depends on what you ask for and what proof you provide, so be explicit in your request about what the family wants.

Without any report, the account simply stays live indefinitely, which is why acting matters: dormant accounts of deceased people are targets for hacking and impersonation.

How to Report a Death to TikTok: Step by Step

1
Gather the account details

You need the exact username (the handle beginning with @) and a link to the profile. Open the profile in the app or at tiktok.com and copy the link from the share menu.

2
Prepare the documents

A death certificate is the core document. Add proof of your relationship to the person, such as a birth or marriage certificate, or the document naming you as executor or administrator of the estate. Photograph or scan them clearly.

3
Submit through TikTok's support form

Use TikTok's feedback and support form at tiktok.com/legal/report/feedback, choosing the closest topic to account issues. State clearly that the account holder has died, give the username and profile link, say whether the family wants memorialisation or removal, and attach the documents.

4
Report the profile in the app as well

On the profile, tap the share arrow, then Report. This creates a second signal inside TikTok's moderation system. On its own it rarely resolves the case, but combined with the support form it speeds things up.

5
Follow up until it is resolved

TikTok's replies can take weeks and sometimes ask for documents you have already sent. Reply to the same email thread, attach everything again, and restate the request. Persistence is normally what closes these cases.

Common Problems

No reply at all

Resubmit the form after two weeks, and vary the topic selection if the first choice went nowhere. Requests written in clear, short sentences with documents attached from the start get faster treatment than long emotional accounts, hard as that is to write in grief.

The account is being impersonated or misused

If someone has taken over the account, or a copycat account is using the person's videos, report it as impersonation instead. Impersonation reports are handled by a faster queue than bereavement requests on most platforms, TikTok included.

You cannot find the account

Search the username from another family member's account, or check the Following list of a close friend. Private accounts are harder to locate from outside; a mutual follower can usually supply the exact handle.

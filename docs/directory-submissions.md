## Long description (about 100 words)

Departed Digital is a London based service that closes or memorialises a deceased person's online accounts on the family's behalf. Families tell us once which accounts they know about, send the death certificate and proof of authority once, and we handle every platform's official process, chase every request, and provide a written record at the end. We never ask for passwords. Packages start at £149. We also publish free step by step guides for families doing it themselves, covering Facebook, Instagram, Apple, Google, email, subscriptions, phone contracts and post, and what to do when a request is not working.

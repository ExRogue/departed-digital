# Link outreach kit

Backlinks are the one organic-traffic ingredient the site does not have yet.
Every target below already publishes a page that signposts bereaved families
to digital-legacy help, so a link to a Departed Digital guide is a genuine
improvement to their page, not a favour. Lead with the guide, not the service.

Send from hello@departed.digital, one at a time, personalised with the page
you are referring to. Expect roughly 1 in 8 to say yes. Ten yeses would put
this site ahead of most competitors on authority.

## Other authority levers (need Steven's own accounts)

- **HARO / Qwoted / Featured.com**: journalists regularly ask for quotes on
  "digital death" and "what happens to accounts when you die". Each quote is
  a news link. Free to sign up; answer two or three requests a week.
- **Reddit and Mumsnet**: r/UKPersonalFinance, r/LegalAdviceUK and Mumsnet
  regularly have "what do I do with my mum's Facebook" threads. A genuinely
  helpful answer with a guide link is welcome; a pitch is not.
- **Google Business Profile**: verify it, then post one guide a week as a
  GBP post. Local pack visibility for "close deceased accounts" searches.
- **YouTube**: three-minute screen recordings of "how to memorialise a
  Facebook account" rank in Google's video carousel with little competition.

# Outreach batch 2: UK councils, hospices and bereavement charities

Researched 7 September 2026. 42 organisations that already publish a page
signposting bereaved families to practical help. 23 have a verified email
address; 19 accept contact only through a web form (paste text provided).

Lesson from batch 1 (The Compassionate Friends): charities and councils with
a "free help only" policy will never list a paid service, so every email
below asks for one thing only: a link to the free guides. No packages, no
prices, no pitch. The service is in the signature and nowhere else.

Send from hello@departed.digital, one at a time, in the morning, 10 to 15 a
day. Log the date in the table. Follow up once after 10 days if silent.

Hello,

I run Departed Digital, a small London based service that helps families close or memorialise a loved one's online accounts after a death.

# Outreach batch 4: independent funeral directors in Greater London

Research date: 10 September 2026. Read-only web research; nothing was sent.

Purpose: partnership offer where the funeral director hands a Departed Digital card or leaflet to families as part of aftercare, and Departed Digital closes or memorialises the online accounts (packages from £149; cases start at https://www.departed.digital/start).

How the list was built: SAIF and NAFD member sites, the Good Funeral Guide London directory, and borough-by-borough searches. Every email, page and detail below was read on the firm's own website, not from a directory. Branches of Dignity, Co-op Funeralcare and Funeral Partners were dropped (see the exclusions note at the end). Firms with an aftercare, bereavement support or useful links page are the warmest leads because the leaflet has a natural home there.

Digital mention: no firm in this batch mentions digital legacy, social media accounts or online accounts anywhere I could find. "No*" means I read the firm's aftercare page specifically and it does not cover it; "No" means the homepage and navigation did not mention it. That gap is the pitch: every aftercare page lists Cruse, SAIFCare and probate, none list what to do with a Facebook or Gmail account.

## The pitch

This is not the free guides ask used for charities and councils. Funeral directors are a revenue channel, so the email offers a partnership: they hand the family a card, we do the work, they receive a referral fee. Three things make it land.

1. **The gap is specific and true.** Their aftercare page lists Cruse, SAIFCare and probate. None of them cover what happens to a Facebook, a Gmail or an Amazon account. Name their page.
2. **The first family is free.** They can judge the work before putting their name near it. This removes all the risk from saying yes.
3. **Nothing to administer.** No forms for their team, no documents, no passwords. One card in the aftercare pack.

Attach or link the two PDFs: the A5 family card and the A4 partner overview at www.departed.digital/partners/aftercare. Send from hello@departed.digital, verify each in Sent, and log the date.

Send order: the 16 firms with an aftercare page first (numbers 1, 2, 6, 11, 12, 16, 17, 20, 21, 24, 25, 28, 31, 33, 34, 39), then the rest, 10 to 12 a day. Follow up once after 10 days, then leave it.

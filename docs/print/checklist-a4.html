Digital accounts to close or memorialise after a death

UK edition, September 2026
Keep with the death certificate

Tick what applies. You do not need to find everything before you start, and you never need a password. Save photos and messages before closing anything, and close the email account last.

Before you close anything

  • Keep the phone charged; do not wipe it
  • Keep the mobile number live for a few months (security codes)
  • Save photos, messages and tributes first
  • Check for a legacy contact (Facebook, Instagram, Apple) or Google's Inactive Account Manager

Social media

  • Facebook memorialise or remove
  • Instagram separate request, even if linked
  • Threads follows Instagram
  • X (Twitter) deactivation only
  • TikTok save the videos first
  • LinkedIn memorialise or close
  • Snapchat, Pinterest, Reddit, YouTube channel support request
  • Dating apps, forums, gaming communities

Email and the accounts that unlock everything

  • Email Gmail, Outlook/Hotmail, Yahoo, BT/Sky/Virgin/TalkTalk: close last
  • Google Account Gmail, Photos, Drive, YouTube, Android
  • Apple Account iCloud, backups, Activation Lock
  • Microsoft account Outlook, OneDrive, Xbox, 365
  • Password manager 1Password, LastPass, Bitwarden, browser

Messaging

  • WhatsApp export chats; deleted after 120 days idle
  • Messenger handled with Facebook
  • Signal, Telegram, iMessage

Money and shopping

  • PayPal balance is part of the estate
  • Amazon Prime, Kindle, Audible, Alexa, gift balance
  • eBay, Etsy, Vinted, Depop open sales, fees, balances
  • Apple and Google app subscriptions
  • Banking, savings, investment and crypto apps via bereavement teams
  • Loyalty points Nectar, Clubcard, Avios, hotels

Subscriptions that keep charging

  • Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, NOW, Apple TV+
  • Spotify, Apple Music, Audible, YouTube Premium
  • iCloud+, Google One, Dropbox, OneDrive
  • Microsoft 365, antivirus, VPN, fitness and dating apps
  • Ring, Nest, Hive, alarm monitoring
  • Newspapers, magazines, recipe boxes, delivery passes

Phone, broadband and TV

  • Mobile contract bereavement team; keep SIM live until done
  • Broadband and landline and the email that came with it
  • Sky, Virgin, BT TV, TV licence

Devices, work and public accounts

  • Phone, tablet, laptop, smartwatch, memory cards, drives
  • Smart speakers and doorbells that still record
  • PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Steam
  • Website or domain name expires, then someone else can register it
  • Blog, YouTube channel, podcast, Patreon, Substack, Etsy shop
  • Professional memberships, portfolio sites

The order to do it in

  1. Stop the money: bank, PayPal, mobile contract, anything charging a relative's card
  2. Secure what is public: memorialise or remove the social media profiles
  3. Save what matters: photos, chats, devices
  4. Close the rest, email last
The two documents you will need again and again: the death certificate, and a birth or marriage certificate or the grant of probate that shows you can act. Nobody should ever ask for a password.

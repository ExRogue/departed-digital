Departed Digital

The answer to "what do we do about their Facebook?"

Families ask you about online accounts in almost every arrangement. Departed Digital is a Maida Vale service that closes or memorialises a person's social media, email, and online accounts on the family's behalf, so you have a complete answer to give, at no cost to your firm.

How it works for a family you refer
  1. The family starts a case at our website in about a minute and pays securely by card.
  2. They send us the death certificate and proof of authority through their private case page. We never ask for passwords.
  3. We handle every platform's official process, follow up until each account is closed or memorialised, and give the family a written record for the estate.

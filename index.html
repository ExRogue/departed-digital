How it works

Three simple steps

You do the first two steps online. We take care of everything after that.

1

Tell us about your loved one

Fill in a short form. It takes about a minute. We only need their name and any accounts you know about, and it is fine if you do not know them all.

2

Choose a package and pay securely

Pick Essential, Standard or Estate on the same page, whichever fits the number of accounts, and pay securely by card.

3

Send us the documents and we handle everything

Upload the death certificate and proof that you can act for them on your private case page. Then we deal with each platform and keep you updated until everything is resolved.

What we do

We handle everything

Every account, every form, every follow up. You send us the details once. We do the rest.

Closing accounts

We submit the request to close each account and see it through until the platform confirms it is done.

Memorial pages

Some platforms can keep a profile as a place to remember someone. If you would rather keep it than close it, we arrange that.

All the paperwork

Every platform asks for different forms and evidence. We prepare and submit it all, so you only have to send things to us once.

All the chasing

Platforms can be slow. We follow up on every request so you never have to chase anyone yourself.

Why we do this

One less thing to carry

When someone dies, the admin does not stop. Their accounts keep sending reminders, birthdays and old photos. We take that part off your hands, quietly and properly, so you can give your attention to the people around you.

Why families trust us

Departed Digital was built around one idea: the online admin after a death should be quietly taken care of, not left to the family. That is the whole service.
